Privacy Policy

App: Shevic  |  Package: com.ag5.frontend  |  Last updated: June 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Shevic ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our mobile application. By using Shevic, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Username and display name chosen by you
  • Email address (used for authentication and account management)
  • Profile data you voluntarily provide

User-Generated Content

  • Videos and images you upload or share within the app
  • YouTube URLs you submit for video processing
  • Collections and playlists you create

Device & Technical Information

  • Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID) used for advertising personalisation
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver notifications
  • IP address, device model, OS version, and app version for diagnostics

Usage Information

  • App interactions, features used, and content viewed
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core app functionality (video generation, sharing, collections)
  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Send push notifications about activity relevant to you (you can opt out in device settings)
  • Store and serve your uploaded media via Firebase Storage
  • Maintain your account data in a PostgreSQL database (hosted on Neon)
  • Display personalised and non-personalised ads via Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded formats)
  • Analyse usage to improve the app

3. Data Storage & Infrastructure

  • Firebase Storage (Google LLC) – stores user-uploaded videos and images. Data may be stored on Google's servers globally.
  • Neon PostgreSQL – stores account data, metadata, and app content in a cloud-hosted relational database.
  • Redis – used transiently for background job queuing; no personal data is retained.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob (AdMob App ID: ca-app-pub-1733912397767008~6132082120) to display ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) for ad personalisation
  • Device and usage data to measure ad performance
  • Location data (if permission granted) for location-based ads

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
AdMob's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications. Your FCM token is stored on our servers solely to deliver notifications. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings. Revoking notification permission does not affect other app functionality.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google LLC – Firebase Storage, FCM, AdMob, and Google Play Services
  • Neon (database hosting) – to store your account and content data
  • Railway / cloud infrastructure providers – to run our backend servers
  • Legal authorities – if required by law or to protect rights and safety

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • Uploaded videos and images are retained until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Ad-related identifiers are managed by Google per their retention policies.
  • Job processing data in Redis is automatically purged after completion.

8. Account Deletion

You have the right to request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

To request account deletion:

  • Use the in-app "Delete Account" option (if available), or
  • Email us at amoghpai2004@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address.

We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Deletion removes your account credentials, uploaded content, and profile data from our systems. Residual data in backups is purged within 90 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Shevic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

11. Security

We use HMAC-signed URLs for media access, HTTPS for all data transmission, and industry-standard security practices. No system is 100% secure; use the app at your own risk.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
